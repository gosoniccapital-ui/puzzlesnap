# Hallmark Anti-Patterns & 57-Gate Slop Checklist

## Core Forbidden Sins
1. **AI-Purple & Magenta Mesh Gradients:** Avoid standard `#8b5cf6 -> #ec4899` mesh blobs that scream AI-generated template. Use curated Haute Couture palettes: Warm Amber & Bronze Gold (`#dfba73`, `#b45309`), Deep Obsidian (`#0c0a09`), Rose Quartz Noir, or Alabaster Ivory.
2. **Centered Boring Hero:** Avoid a plain centered title + single sentence + two generic buttons. Use asymmetric editorial layouts with hero photography, tactile metadata pills, and micro-metrics.
3. **Card Uniformity:** Do not arrange all cards in identical cookie-cutter rectangles. Use hierarchy: lead hero card, staggered gallery, interactive hover peek, and dynamic badges.
4. **Italic Headers:** No `<em>word</em>` italic emphasis inside roman headings.
5. **Missing States:** Buttons that have no hover lift, no focus ring, no active press depth (`active:scale-[0.98]`).
6. **Flat Borders:** Plain 1px solid borders look cheap. Use subtle hairlines with gradient fades (`border border-amber-500/20 shadow-xl shadow-amber-950/10`).
